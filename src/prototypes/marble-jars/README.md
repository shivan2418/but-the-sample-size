# PROTOTYPE, throwaway: marble jars, round 3

Answers [Marble jars in Storybook: D rebuilt, and fresh takes](https://github.com/shivan2418/but-the-sample-size/issues/15). Round 1 and 2 (variants A–D as one HTML page) are on [`claude/jar-prototype-vn9bzk`](https://github.com/shivan2418/but-the-sample-size/tree/claude/jar-prototype-vn9bzk/prototypes/marble-jars).

Run `pnpm storybook` and open **Prototypes / marble-jars**. Stories open at phone width. Every story has `palette` (red/blue or neutral), `speed` (multiplies every duration) and, where there's a dot chart, `axisRange` (0–100% or 30–80%) as controls.

| Variant              | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Stories                                        |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **D · Side jar**     | Round 2's D rebuilt: tactile to abstract in five skippable steps. The jar stands upright at the left. Marbles come out of its mouth sideways into a tray on the right. Timings are about half of round 2's (one marble 0.6 s, 10 ≈ 1 s, 100 ≈ 1.5 s). Step 4 zooms out to a jar 10× in each direction.                                                           | One per step                                   |
| **E · Pour**         | A rethink with no handfuls and no dots. Both jars tip out together, slowly at first (single marbles), then ten times faster every 1.6 s. A line traces "% red so far" on a log scale of marbles counted, against the band 95% of pours stay inside. The small jar runs dry at 1,000, where its line lands exactly on 55%. "Stop at" is the reader's sample size. | Fresh; after 8 pours                           |
| **F · Twenty hands** | A rethink with no sequence. Twenty people grab at once, so the spread is visible as a 5 × 4 wall of trays. "Line them up" sorts the trays by share and squeezes each into a dot on a strip. Jar and handful size are toggles, and the previous wall stays as hollow dots for comparison.                                                                         | Handfuls of 10; 100 from the big jar, lined up |

`model.ts` holds the shared draws (exact, without replacement) and the margin of error. `Frame.svelte` mocks the page around the widget.
